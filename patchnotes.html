13 September 2026

Radiant Wilds, balance pass 2

  • Bloodletter stays in S, since the cooldown change did not stop it from resetting out of a losing fight.
  • Hallowfall moves ahead of Redemption as the default healer because the shield now holds through a full dive.
  • Hoarfrost drops to B, as control alone does not kill a duo with a healer.

3 August 2026

Radiant Wilds launch

  • Both pages were rebuilt from scratch after the gate reward change pulled more players into 2v2.
  • Double Bloodletter enters at S thanks to how fast it closes a fight before any heal matters.
  • Longbow falls to C since nothing about it survives a dive comp.

19 June 2026

Hotfix

  • Dagger mobility reverted, so no comp moved on either page.
