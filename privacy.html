Privacy

What this site collects about you

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do here is recorded or sent anywhere by this site.

What happens when you open a page

Three things are requested from elsewhere, and each one can see your IP address the way any website you visit can:

  • Google Fonts, for the two typefaces the site uses.
  • GitHub, for the recorded kill numbers on the matchups page.
  • Your host, which like any web server may keep access logs.

None of these are asked to identify you, and none of them are given anything beyond the ordinary request itself.

The Albion data

The recorded numbers come from Albion Online's public kill feed. That is game data about in game characters, not about you, and is collected whether or not anyone visits this site.

Getting in touch

Questions about any of this, or a request to have something removed, go to Discord.
