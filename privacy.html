Privacy

What this site collects about you

No accounts, no forms, no advertising and nothing that follows you around. The one thing that is measured is how many people read which page, and that is counted in a way that cannot pick you out.

Counting readers

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is there to answer one question: is anyone reading this. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not fingerprint your device, so there is no identifier tying one visit of yours to the next.

What it records for a page view is the page address, the page you came from, your country, and rough browser, operating system and device type. Your IP address is used only in the moment, to work out the country and to spot duplicate hits, and is never written down. None of it is sold, shared for advertising, or used to build a profile.

If you would rather not be counted at all, any ad or tracker blocker stops the beacon, and the site works exactly the same without it.

The Albion data

The recorded numbers come from Albion Online's public kill feed. That is game data about in game characters, not about you, and is collected whether or not anyone visits this site.
