Cookies

This site sets no cookies

Not one. Nothing is stored in your browser, nothing is read back, and there is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

What that means in practice

No session is kept, nothing you click is remembered between visits, and you cannot be recognised on a return visit. Every visitor is served the same pages.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google and one data file from GitHub. Neither sets a cookie through this site. Should that ever change, this page changes with it and a consent banner goes up before anything is stored.
